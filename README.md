## Why a Visa card may be declined

A declined ChatGPT Plus payment does not necessarily mean the card is invalid. OpenAI’s troubleshooting guidance points to several common causes, including incorrect payment details, billing-address problems, bank restrictions, unsupported regions, and security checks.

### 1. International payments are disabled

Many banks disable international transactions by default, especially for debit cards. Check your banking app for settings such as:

- International transactions
- Online transactions
- E-commerce payments
- Recurring payments
- Foreign-currency transactions

Turn on only the setting you actually need. If your bank requires confirmation by SMS or app notification, complete that step before trying again.

### 2. The billing address does not match

Enter the billing address associated with the Visa card. Do not use a random address simply because the checkout page displays a foreign currency or a different country.

A mismatch between the cardholder address and the submitted billing details can trigger an automatic decline. OpenAI specifically recommends checking payment details, billing address, and card expiration information when a card payment fails.

### 3. Your card or region is unsupported

The customer and the card issuer generally need to be located in supported countries or regions. OpenAI says payments may be declined when either the user or the card issuer is outside the supported regions.

This is one reason a Visa card can work for other international websites but fail on ChatGPT. The card network is only one part of the transaction. The merchant’s regional availability rules still apply.

### 4. Bank fraud protection blocks the transaction

Banks sometimes block unfamiliar merchants, recurring subscriptions, or transactions that appear to originate from a different country. If the payment is declined immediately, contact the card issuer and ask whether the merchant transaction was blocked.

Do not keep clicking the payment button repeatedly. Multiple failed attempts can create temporary authorization holds or make the bank’s fraud system more cautious.

### 5. Additional verification was not completed

Some transactions require 3D Secure, app approval, or another form of authentication. In the European Economic Area, Strong Customer Authentication may appear during purchase or renewal. Follow the verification prompt and keep the payment page open until the bank completes the check.
